How to read this dashboard. Two views over the same dataset.
  • Comparison matrix — rows are a unified taxonomy (10 categories), columns are the six banks. Cells list each bank's native product name(s) under that category. "Not public" cells are publicly undisclosed at this depth, not necessarily absent.
  • Per-bank deep dives — collapsible cards. Each card preserves the bank's own native taxonomy, then maps each product into the unified categories so you can see how each bank positions and bundles its catalogue.
  • Filters (sticky bar above) — narrow by bank, by unified category, or by client segment. Search box highlights matches across both views.
  • Caveats — pricing is almost always "Not public"; bespoke / FI-tier products often aren't marketed publicly; bank websites change frequently. This is a point-in-time snapshot.
