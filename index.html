Transaction Banking Operating Map

A shared operating model for Cash Management, Trade & Working Capital, and Foreign Exchange. Process is the canvas; revenue zones are the punctuation. Channels & Connectivity (web portal, mobile app, host-to-host, APIs) sit underneath all three lanes and are not modelled separately.

Internal stakeholder artefact Last updated 25 May 2026
Revenue

Line logic

  • Solid line: client money movement
  • Dashed line: bank funding, hedging, or risk position
  • Thin: lower relative flow
  • Thick: higher relative flow

Revenue types

  • $Net Interest Income — the dollars earned on the spread (NIM) between what the bank pays for deposits and what it earns lending or investing them; includes interest on short-term trade and working-capital loans
  • $Transaction fees — a few cents per payment, a few dollars per cross-border transfer, plus fees on guarantees and LC confirmations
  • $FX spread — the gap between the bank's wholesale rate and the rate quoted to the customer on every conversion

Lane colours

  • Cash Management
  • Trade & Working Capital
  • Foreign Exchange (FX)

Reading the map

  • Revenue markers sit exactly where value is captured.
  • Money flows left to right from client need to business outcome.
  • Cross-lane paths show when payments, trade, and FX reinforce each other.
